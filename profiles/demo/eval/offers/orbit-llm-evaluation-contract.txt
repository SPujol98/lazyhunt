LLM Evaluation Engineer (contract, remote EU)

Orbit Assist (orbit-assist.example.com) builds customer-support copilots. We need an engineer for a six-month contract to design the evaluation harness that decides whether a new prompt or model goes live.

Scope
- Build datasets and automatic checks for answer quality, groundedness and tone.
- Implement regression suites in Python that run in CI on every prompt change.
- Analyse failure cases and propose fixes to the retrieval and prompting layers.
- Document the evaluation method for the wider engineering team.

Requirements
- Good Python and pytest; experience calling LLM APIs.
- Understanding of retrieval-augmented generation and of metrics for retrieval and generation.
- Written English; you will report weekly to the head of product.
- 1-3 years of related experience. A degree or equivalent practical experience.

Nice to have
- Experience with annotation tools or human evaluation protocols.
- Spanish or French for reading customer transcripts.

Conditions
- Fully remote for residents of the EU.
- Day rate between 220 and 280 EUR as a freelancer, invoiced monthly.
- Possible extension or permanent offer after the contract.
- No fixed hours: deliverables are agreed every two weeks.

This is a contract position, not an employee role, so benefits are not included. If you are comfortable invoicing as a self-employed person in Spain, apply with your CV and a short note about a similar project.
