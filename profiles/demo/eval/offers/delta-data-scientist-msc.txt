Data Scientist (Barcelona, hybrid)

Delta Biometrics (delta-biometrics.example.com) analyses sensor data from wearable devices for clinical research. We are looking for a data scientist to join our research group in Barcelona.

Responsibilities
- Develop statistical and machine learning models on physiological signals.
- Design experiments and validate them with clinical partners.
- Write Python code for data pipelines and reproducible analyses.
- Prepare figures and sections for scientific publications.

Requirements
- Master's degree (MSc) in statistics, mathematics, physics, bioengineering or a closely related discipline is mandatory.
- 1-2 years of experience with Python, scikit-learn and signal processing.
- Solid foundations in probability and statistics.
- Fluent English; Spanish is useful.
- Interest in biomedical research.

Nice to have
- A PhD or published papers.
- Experience with R.

We offer
- Hybrid work, two days a week in our Barcelona lab.
- 33,000 - 38,000 EUR gross per year.
- Time for conferences and a collaborative research culture.
- A permanent contract after the probation period.

Candidates without a Master's degree cannot be considered for this position, as the qualification is a requirement of the research grants that fund the role.
