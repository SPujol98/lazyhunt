Machine Learning Engineer (remote, Europe)

Cobalt Insight (cobalt-insight.example.com) is a remote-first company that builds forecasting tools for energy retailers. We are growing the ML team and are looking for an engineer with a couple of years of hands-on experience.

Responsibilities
- Develop and deploy ML models for demand forecasting and anomaly detection.
- Build data pipelines in Python and SQL and keep them reliable.
- Package models as services and deploy them on AWS (ECS, S3, CloudWatch).
- Monitor models in production and iterate with the data scientists.

Requirements
- 2-3 years of experience in machine learning or data engineering roles.
- Strong Python, pandas and scikit-learn; basic knowledge of PyTorch.
- Hands-on experience with a cloud platform, ideally AWS.
- Good written and spoken English.
- A degree in a quantitative field or equivalent practical experience.

Nice to have
- Experience with MLflow or similar experiment tracking.
- Knowledge of time series methods.

What we offer
- Fully remote work from any country in the EU, with a yearly team meeting in Lisbon.
- 40,000 - 48,000 EUR gross per year.
- Home-office budget, 28 days of holiday and a conference allowance.
- A supportive team that documents everything.

If you meet most of these points we would like to hear from you, even if you do not tick every box.
