Data Analyst / BI Developer (Barcelona, hybrid)

Requirements
- 2 years of experience in an analyst or BI role.
- Advanced SQL and good command of Power BI (DAX, data modelling).
- Python or R for data cleaning is a plus.
- Excellent communication in Spanish; business English.
- Degree in economics, engineering, statistics or a similar field.

We offer
- Hybrid schedule with two days a week in our Barcelona offices.
- Salary of 30,000 - 36,000 EUR gross per year plus a performance bonus.
- Employee discount in our stores, health insurance and intensive summer hours.
- Career path towards analytics engineering.

The daily core of this role is reporting and dashboards, with Python as occasional support. We will review applications as they arrive.
