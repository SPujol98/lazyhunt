Junior RAG Engineer (remote, Spain)

Lumen Search Labs (lumen-search.example.com) is a small product team building a document question-answering service for public libraries and archives. We are looking for a junior engineer to join the retrieval team.

What you will do
- Build and improve a retrieval-augmented generation pipeline: chunking, embeddings, hybrid search and reranking.
- Write evaluation scripts to measure retrieval quality (recall@k, answer faithfulness) and track regressions.
- Expose the pipeline through a FastAPI service backed by PostgreSQL with pgvector.
- Work with the team on prompts and guardrails for the LLM layer.

What we ask for
- Strong Python; you have shipped at least one personal or professional project using LLMs or search.
- Basic SQL and Docker.
- Written and spoken English (B2 is enough). Spanish is welcome, not required.
- Vocational training, a bootcamp or a degree in a related field, or equivalent practical experience. Up to 2 years of experience is plenty: we value learning speed and personal projects.

Nice to have
- Experience with evaluation of retrieval systems.
- Some familiarity with a cloud provider.

We offer
- 100% remote work from anywhere in Spain, with optional co-working budget.
- Flexible hours, 25 days of holiday and a learning budget.
- A permanent contract with a salary between 28,000 and 34,000 EUR gross per year.
- A friendly team that reviews every pull request together.

Send us your CV and a link to a project you are proud of. Applications from people changing careers into data and AI are especially welcome.
