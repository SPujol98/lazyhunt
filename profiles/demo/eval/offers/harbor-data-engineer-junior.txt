Junior Data Engineer, Logistics Analytics (Remote within Spain)

Harbor Freight Tech (harbor-freight.example.com) moves containers and parcels across southern Europe and we need clean data to run it. The analytics platform team is hiring a junior data engineer, fully remote for candidates living in Spain.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain batch data pipelines in Python and SQL that load shipment events into a warehouse.
- Clean, validate and document datasets used by operations and planning teams.
- Create small APIs and dashboards' data feeds that expose delivery metrics.
- Monitor pipeline health and fix data quality issues together with the operations team.

Requirements
- Solid Python and SQL (PostgreSQL preferred).
- Understanding of ETL concepts; experience from a bootcamp, an internship or a personal project is enough.
- Working proficiency in English; we use it in all meetings.
- Willingness to learn orchestration tools such as Airflow.
- Up to 2 years of experience. Higher vocational training or a degree in a technical field, or equivalent experience.

A plus
- Previous work with logistics, transport or operations data.
- Basic knowledge of Docker.

What we offer
- Remote work in Spain, with a team meetup twice a year.
- 29,000 - 35,000 EUR gross per year and an annual bonus.
- 25 days of holiday, flexible schedule and a home-office allowance.
- Mentoring from an experienced data engineer who will review your first pipelines.

Apply with your CV in English or Spanish.
