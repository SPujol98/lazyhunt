Backend Engineer, Python (hybrid Barcelona)

Quanta Mobility (quanta-mobility.example.com) is a Barcelona scale-up that connects electric scooters with fleet operators. Our platform team needs a backend engineer to help us scale the API layer.

Responsibilities
- Develop and operate FastAPI services that handle telemetry from thousands of vehicles.
- Design PostgreSQL schemas and optimise queries under load.
- Contribute to our infrastructure as code (Terraform) and deploy to Kubernetes on a managed cloud.
- Participate in on-call rotations after an initial onboarding period.

Requirements
- 3+ years of experience developing Python backends in production.
- Experience with Docker and CI/CD pipelines; knowledge of Kubernetes is valued.
- Good SQL and an understanding of REST design.
- Spanish and professional English.
- Degree in computer science or equivalent experience.

Nice to have
- Experience with message brokers (Kafka, RabbitMQ).
- Metrics and tracing with Prometheus and Grafana.

We offer
- Hybrid work: three days a week at our office in Barcelona.
- 38,000 - 46,000 EUR gross per year, equity programme and health insurance.
- A direct impact on a product with growing traffic.

Candidates with slightly less than three years who have built something comparable are welcome to apply.
