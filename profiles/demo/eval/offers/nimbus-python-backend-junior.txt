Python Backend Developer - Junior/Mid (Barcelona)

Nimbus Ledger (nimbus-ledger.example.com) builds invoicing software for freelancers and very small companies. Our engineering team is based in Barcelona and we are growing the backend squad.

Your day to day
- Design and maintain REST APIs in Python with FastAPI.
- Model data in PostgreSQL, write migrations and optimise slow queries.
- Write automated tests with pytest and keep the CI pipeline green.
- Integrate third-party services (payments, e-mail, accounting exports).
- Take part in code reviews and in weekly planning.

Requirements
- 1 to 3 years of professional experience with Python backends (internships and freelance work count).
- Good knowledge of SQL and relational modelling.
- Git, Docker and basic Linux skills.
- Spanish and English; Catalan is a plus.
- A degree or higher vocational diploma in computer science, or similar training.

Nice to have
- Metrics and observability (Prometheus, Grafana).
- Experience with message queues.

What we offer
- Hybrid model in our Barcelona office: three days on site, two remote.
- Salary 30,000 - 38,000 EUR gross per year depending on experience.
- Health insurance, flexible hours and a yearly training budget.
- A calm engineering culture without on-call duties for juniors.

Please apply with your CV. We will reply to every application within two weeks, and the first interview is a relaxed technical conversation about a project you built.
