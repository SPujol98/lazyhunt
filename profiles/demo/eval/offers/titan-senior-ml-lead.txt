Senior Machine Learning Engineer / Team Lead (Barcelona)

- Design and train deep learning models for fraud detection at scale.

Requirements
- 7+ years of experience in machine learning, with at least 3 years leading engineers.
- Proven track record training and deploying large models in production.
- Expert Python, PyTorch and distributed training.
- Experience with Kubernetes and cloud platforms.
- MSc or PhD in computer science, mathematics or physics.
- Fluent English and Spanish.

We offer

- 70,000 - 90,000 EUR gross per year plus bonus and stock options.
- Conference budget and a relocation package if needed.

This role is not suitable for candidates at the start of their career: we expect a senior profile with people management experience from day one.
